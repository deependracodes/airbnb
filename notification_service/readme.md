# Steps to generate a gmail app password
  - MAIL_USER: Enter your full Gmail address (for example, yourname@gmail.com).
  - MAIL_PASS: Generate a 16-character App Password from your Google Account:
  - Go to your Google Account settings (My Account > Security).
  - Ensure 2-Step Verification is turned on for your account
  - Search for App passwords in the search bar
  - Create a project name and copy the password.
